Optimized control-variate comparison — fixed analysis specification
12 November 2026. Recorded before inspecting the control-variate outcomes.
This is a new, post hoc analysis specification, not historical preregistration.

Data and scope
Use all 200 existing primary zero-source chains at the original eight mass
endpoints. Recheck the raw hashes and the conditional library pin. No new
sampler runs, random draws, bootstrap draws, or LaTeX/PDF execution are allowed.
The manuscript input is the verified 849-line/858-line latest patched source
pair in novelty_positioning_20261112/patched. Do not substitute the different
3745-line Downloads/NNLFT/main.tex.

Comparator
Use uniform-mode Schwinger–Dyson/Stein identities h'(t)-U_psi'(t)h(t), which
are accessible from the retained (u,S2,S3,Gp) data. This implements the published
zero-mean covariance-optimization strategy in the uniform direction; it is not
a reproduction of an unavailable full-site control basis or a neural control
variate. Do not use conditional quadrature or conditional moments to fit the
comparator. Quadrature remains the independently evaluated reference method.

Let X=L*u and t=3*X. The conditional potential is
 U_psi(t) = a*t^4 + b*t^2 + c*t,
 a=lambda/81,
 b=(m0sq*L^4/2+6*lambda*S2)/(9*L^2),
 c=4*lambda*S3/(3*L), d=b-1/2.
Use normalized probabilists' Hermite polynomials h_j=He_j/sqrt(j!).
Define A_j=h_j'-(4*a*t^3+2*b*t+c)*h_j.
Every background multiplier below is held fixed when differentiating t.

Basis order (all controls have exact conditional expectation zero):
 1–4: A_j, j=1,3,5,7;
 5–8: c*A_j, j=0,2,4,6;
 9–12: d*A_j, j=1,3,5,7;
 13–16: c^2*A_j, j=1,3,5,7;
 17–20: c*d*A_j, j=0,2,4,6;
 21–24: c^3*A_j, j=0,2,4,6.
The primary comparator is SD24 with standardized-covariance ridge rho=1e-6.
Report SD4, SD8, SD12 at the same rho as basis checks, plus SD24 with rho=1e-4
and 1e-2 as fixed regularization checks. Do not select a basis/ridge from test
performance or report a selected best result as prechosen.

Optimization and evaluation
The target vector is (X^2,X^4,Gp). Fit its three coefficient vectors by minimizing
centered per-configuration squared residuals plus rho times the squared norm
of the coefficients on standardized controls. Covariances come from all raw
training rows, stored as block sums/cross-products. Do not demean controls on
the evaluation data: their population expectations are known to be zero.

Use two-fold cross-fitting by independent chains. Within each stage/endpoint,
chain-index parity defines the folds, balancing both stages. Each chain is
evaluated only with coefficients trained on the opposite fold. Training counts
are 4,4,13,29 chains per endpoint/fold at L=12,16,24,32. All rows are evaluated
once as held-out observations; generation cost is common to all methods.

Inference and uncertainty
Recompute each method's mass and coupling at each endpoint and interpolate
with its own measured masses to z=3. Preserve raw/conditional/CV pairing.
For stratified delete-block jackknife, refit the coefficients and the training
scales after each deletion, then recompute mass, coupling and interpolation.
Use the original eight blocks per chain for the primary errors. Also delete
groups corresponding to 4,2,1 blocks per chain, retaining the same row-level
training objective, to check block sensitivity. One block means whole-chain
deletion. Do not freeze coefficients when calculating the primary CV errors.
Report paired differences against conditional quadrature and variance ratios;
do not replace the manuscript's adopted primary errors with diagnostic errors.

Cost
Reprocess raw and conditional moments and SD24 features on the same rows in one
local process. Time data loading/hashing separately. Include the SD row-level
training covariance accumulation in feature-pass timings; report coefficient
fit and uncertainty-refit costs separately. Repeat raw/conditional/SD24 timing
on deterministic evenly spaced 65,536-row subsets at every endpoint. Keep
historical HIPSTER generation timers separate; do not invent a same-hardware
generation-plus-analysis speedup. No current hardware simulation is permitted.

Interpretation
Report whichever estimator wins or ties, including any worse CV results. This
comparison tests the specified optimized SD family, not all possible control
variates. Existing primary physics, interpolation and cutoff-model boundaries
remain in force. Reassess novelty on the measured result, not the desired result.
